# Code of Conduct

Halcyon is a technical project. Participation does not require adherence to
any political, ideological, religious, moral, or philosophical position.

## Conduct

- Keep discussions relevant to the development and use of Halcyon.
- Technical disagreement, criticism, and questioning of architectural decisions
  are welcome.
- Personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination, and unauthorized
  disclosure of private information are unacceptable.
- Project channels must not be used for unrelated political campaigning,
  ideological advocacy, or demands that participants publicly declare or
  justify their personal beliefs.

## Scope and moderation

These rules apply equally to everyone, including the maintainer and sponsors.

Personal beliefs and unrelated lawful activities outside the project are not
subject to this policy.

The maintainer may close or remove off-topic discussions and restrict
participation in response to abusive or disruptive conduct.

Participation is judged by conduct within the project, not by political
affiliation, nationality, personal identity, or private convictions.
