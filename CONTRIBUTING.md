# Contributing to Halcyon

Halcyon is currently developed and maintained exclusively by its original creator.

## Feedback

Bug reports, feature requests, and technical discussions are welcome.

Submissions do not guarantee acceptance, implementation, or a response.
For security-sensitive reports, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather
than disclosing vulnerability details through public Issues.

## Discussion scope

Halcyon is a technical project. Discussions should remain relevant to its
development and use. Technical criticism and questions about architectural
decisions are welcome.

Political debates unrelated to the project, personal attacks, harassment, and
discriminatory remarks are outside the scope of this community. Off-topic
discussions may be closed, and harassment or repeated disruption may result
in restrictions on participation. These rules apply equally to everyone.
See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for the conduct policy.

For the project's approach to technical questions and responses, see
[How are technical questions addressed?](docs/guide/faq.md#how-are-technical-questions-addressed).

## Development

The original project's architecture, specifications, implementation, and
technical direction remain under the sole authority of its maintainer.

Any accepted changes must comply with Halcyon's existing Constitution,
specifications, and engineering rules.

Pull requests are not accepted at this time. There is no scheduled date
for changing this policy.

## Forks

Everyone is free to fork, modify, and distribute Halcyon's own code under
the terms of the [MIT license](LICENSE). Bundled third-party code and assets
retain their applicable license terms and notices.
