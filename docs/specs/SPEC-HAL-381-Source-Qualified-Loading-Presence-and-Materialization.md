### 3.1 Producer-qualified proxy payload

A proxy input must retain all of the following as one immutable, bounded source association:

- Exact opaque32-byte canonical original SourceBinding digest, source revision/generation and concrete original root/resource identity; separate proxy content identity/revision and hash of the actual derived proxy bytes with its explicitly identified upstream algorithm. For FAB011 the original binding is BLAKE3; its bytes are never compared to a PBR HDR u64/revision as if those were one identity domain.
- Attributable producer and derivation recipe/version plus the qualification evidence required by the source producer's separately accepted policy. A hash binds bytes; it does not prove derivation, geometric fidelity or a correct normal/material certificate.
- Actual proxy vertices/indexed triangles or separately selected genuine surface representation, complete topology/counts, immutable storage and checked retained/transient costs. An AABB may bound these data but cannot replace the claimed model silhouette.
- Model-space origin-to-proxy transform/frame, true finite extents, vertex/triangle counts, units and source-to-current-model placement association. Finite, representable projection and consistent handedness/depth basis must be checked through existing owners. Approximation and unknown fidelity remain explicit; rendering supplies no physical-fit claim.
- Qualified geometric normals from actual nondegenerate proxy triangles and their interpretation, transform/frame and source association. Closure-04 computes the face normal and camera direction in the same checked canonical render-relative frame under10.3.5; the rigid camera's ideal normalized dot product is the view-space rim quantity, without another camera state. The vectors must be finite/nonzero. A missing qualified normal refuses that primitive/proxy; the `(0,0,1)` packing filler is not proof. These are proxy geometric data, not substituted full-PBR shading normals. Original material, textures, UVs, signed Mikk and R024 frames remain unchanged.

Validation must not require completion of unrelated full normal/PBR proof before the proxy can render. It must nevertheless establish its own geometry/provenance eligibility. Missing, stale, invalid or unprovable proxy data refuse the optional preview only and retain the original loader/current-or-compatible-last-good behavior. There is no fabricated substitute.

The upstream source owner qualifies geometric derivation and supplies its attributable association proof/receipt. HAL independently checks geometry, transform/frame, actual costs and Context/resource/request/target/lease identity; it does not promote a digest or producer label into mathematical provenance. The resulting HAL-issued preview lease retains the immutable claim and actual admitted resource. A scoped typed application-provided original-source association binds that preview and the eventual independently admitted full PBR at the exact Viewport request/publication seam. HAL compares the actual association, selection/request, Context, mount, view, target, sample count and generations; any mismatch refuses the optional pair before changing its presentation. The association conveys no semantic product/part authority. It cannot bypass ordinary `request_pbr_scene`/`replace_pbr_scene` admission or infer which independent source the application intended.
