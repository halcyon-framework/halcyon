# SPEC-MC-000: Mastery & Conditioning — Semantic Authority Training Framework

**Status:** Draft (Binding upon adoption)  
**Series:** SPEC-MC  
**Layer:** Cognitive / Architectural Reasoning Discipline  
**Owner:** Core Architecture (Human Operator)  
**Applies To:** All MC sessions, drills, survival zones, and scoring events  
**Out of Scope:** Runtime systems, rendering, UI, storage, performance, tooling

---

## 1. Purpose

This SPEC establishes a formal framework for **training semantic mastery** in complex technical domains.

The goal is not knowledge accumulation.  
The goal is **internal model correction** under pressure.

SPEC-MC defines a controlled environment where:
- Meaning is treated as a **load-bearing structure**.
- Ambiguity is eliminated by constraint.
- Incorrect internal models are exposed, scored, and corrected.

This series exists to condition the human operator to:
- Think in **authority boundaries**.
- Distinguish **meaning** from description.
- Operate under irreversible constraints without semantic drift.

---

## 2. Definition: Meaning (Binding)

Within SPEC-MC, **meaning** is defined as:

> The authoritative interpretation that determines which actions are valid and which are forbidden inside a domain.

Meaning is:
- Not opinion.
- Not wording.
- Not intention.
- Not explanation.

Meaning is **decision power**.

If two components disagree and one proceeds, that component holds meaning.

---

## 3. Semantic Authority (Binding)

A component (human or system) is a **semantic authority** if and only if:

- It produces final decisions for a domain.
- No external actor may reinterpret its outputs.
- Its decisions are not advisory.
- Its outputs are not hints.
- Its outputs terminate debate.

Semantic authority is **exclusive** and **non-shareable**.

Any system that infers, decorates, “improves,” or second-guesses an authority is violating the domain.

---

## 4. Conditioning Mode (“Survival Zone”)

An MC session is a **survival zone**.

Inside a survival zone:
- There is no persona.
- There is no coaching.
- There is no emotional scaffolding.
- There is no negotiation of rules mid-session.

Only the following exist:
- The domain.
- The governing invariants.
- The operator’s reasoning.

Failure is allowed.  
Escape is not.

---

## 5. Scoring System (Binding)

All MC sessions use **explicit scoring**.

### 5.1 Score Units

- **+1** — Correct identification of semantic authority.
- **+1** — Correct rejection of invalid reinterpretation.
- **+1** — Correct action derived from authority.
- **−1** — Ambiguous reasoning.
- **−2** — Authority confusion.
- **−3** — Post-hoc rationalization.
- **−5** — Semantic drift (changing meaning to fit outcome).

### 5.2 Score Effects

- Scores are cumulative within a session.
- Scores are immutable once assigned.
- Scores are reported explicitly.

A session ends when:
- The target concept is mastered (net positive score with no unresolved violations), or
- The operator reaches structural failure (persistent negative score).

---

## 6. Failure Definition (Non-Negotiable)

Failure is not “being wrong.”

Failure is:
- Defending an incorrect meaning.
- Reframing the domain to avoid loss.
- Introducing meta-arguments.
- Appealing to intent over authority.

Failure triggers:
- Immediate halt.
- Explicit identification of the broken assumption.
- Restart from the last stable invariant.

---

## 7. Non-Goals

SPEC-MC explicitly does **not** aim to:
- Be friendly.
- Be motivational.
- Be comprehensive.
- Be comfortable.
- Be efficient.

MC optimizes for **clarity through pressure**, not throughput.

---

## 8. Relationship to Halcyon

SPEC-MC is **orthogonal** to Halcyon’s runtime architecture.

However:
- All Halcyon SPEC language around *semantic authority*, *ownership*, *truth*, and *meaning* is grounded here.
- MC exists to train the human operator to **think in the same invariants Halcyon enforces in code**.

MC is the **human-side enforcement layer**.

---

## 9. Entry Conditions

An MC session may only begin when:
- The operator explicitly opts in.
- The target domain is named.
- The scoring rules are acknowledged.

Once started:
- The session runs to completion.
- No partial exits.
- No tone negotiation.

---

## 10. Exit Conditions

A session ends when:
- The operator demonstrates stable, repeatable reasoning under constraint, or
- The operator’s model collapses and requires a rebuild at a lower abstraction level.

Both outcomes are valid.

---

SPEC-MC-000 is **constitutional**.

All SPEC-MC documents:
- Must comply with this framework.
- May not weaken scoring.
- May not soften failure definitions.
- May not introduce ambiguity around meaning.

Violations invalidate the session.
